# Instagram image enhancement

Tool: built-in imagegen; one faithful restoration per original post.

Prompt: Enhance and upscale the exact original image for a professional architecture portfolio. Improve detail, compression blur, noise, exposure and subtle natural colour/contrast. Preserve composition, proportions, presentation layout, architectural/model geometry, people and identities, objects, line patterns, lighting direction and existing text. Do not add or remove content or invent features. Keep the full source composition so the existing borderless landscape crops remain consistent.

Originals remain in `dist/assets/instagram/`; enhanced images live in its `enhanced/` subdirectory.
